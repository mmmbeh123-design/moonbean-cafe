Privacy Policy

Moonbean Café · Last updated November 6, 2026

The short version

Moonbean Café does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases.

What the game saves

Your game progress (tips, recipe levels, cats, settings and theme) is saved only on your own device, in your browser's local storage. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it.

Third-party services

  • Google Fonts: the game loads its fonts from Google's font servers. Like any website request, this shares your device's IP address with Google. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub Pages: the game is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Permissions

The game only uses vibration for small haptic effects, which you can switch off in Settings. It does not use your location, camera, microphone or contacts.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions? Open an issue at github.com/mmmbeh123-design/moonbean-cafe/issues.
